Manus Essential Medicines Last-Mile Supply Chain Pilot

An introduction to the team’s pre-feasibility working proposal, version 1.0, dated 20 October 2026. The proposal is the controlling source for operating assumptions; no implementation or funding is confirmed.

Purpose & scope
Reduce stockouts in outer health facilities through regular delivery, stock and ordering routines, quality checks and staff support. Start with three facilities, expanding to six only when safety, supply and funding allow.
A 24-month pilot
Four months of assessment and preparation, followed by 20 months of phased operation. Confirm facilities, routes, baseline records, supply agreements and usable operating funds before regular delivery.
Medicine supply & the hub
Use the designated public supply pathway. Track 20 approved essential medicine items and strengths, initially non-refrigerated. Lorengau checks stock, calculates replenishment, packs and dispatches.
Transport & payment
Compare existing shipping capacity with qualified local crewed charters. Two visits per month are a planning baseline, not a fixed 14-day promise. Verify voyage, fuel and receipt records for monthly payment; confirm available funds before departure.
Performance & evidence
The primary outcome is the stockout-day rate across fixed facility–medicine observations. A 30% relative reduction is a provisional planning target. Assess baseline, monthly reports, quarterly counts, a midterm review and a final evaluation.
Planning budget
PGK 2,356,810 for 24 months in the 20 October 2026 working draft. This is an estimate before local quotations; regular medicine purchases and existing public staff and facilities are separate. Do not treat it as secured funding or total health-service cost.
Safety & continuity
The captain makes the final sailing decision. Use suitable vessels, protected cargo, verified handovers and backup transport. Plan next-year funding, responsible staff and contracts before the pilot ends.
What is outside the pilot
No hospital ship, emergency patient transport, onboard library, large-scale new medicine procurement system or vessel construction. Partners, route contracts and funding remain unconfirmed.

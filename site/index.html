01 / THE PLACEManus Province, Papua New Guinea

An ocean between us.
Care within reach.

For health facilities across an island province, getting medicine onto a shelf takes more than a shipment.

It takes available stock, a reliable sea connection, safe storage and someone ready to receive it.

Our proposal starts in Lorengau, Manus Province. We want to connect these everyday tasks into a dependable supply chain, so that distance is less likely to interrupt care.

03 / OUR APPROACHOne system, working together

Connected by sea.sea, care, people and purpose.

Reliable care is built
between deliveries.

We propose four connected areas of work. Each supports the next, from a planned departure to a well-managed medicine shelf.

01
REGULAR DELIVERY

A route people
can rely on.

Compare existing shipping services and qualified local crewed charters. Plan handovers, weather contingencies and backup transport around the needs of each facility.

Use existing capacity before considering vessel ownership.
02
STOCK & ORDERING

Know what’s there.
Know what’s next.

Connect stock records, demand estimates and ordering schedules. Work with existing systems where available, with paper and phone alternatives when connectivity fails.

A practical reporting routine, grounded in local capacity.
03
QUALITY & HANDOVER

Arriving is only
half the promise.

Medicines must arrive in usable condition. Protect them from damage, check each handover, and record storage conditions and expiry dates.

Quality checks continue from dispatch to the shelf.
04
PEOPLE & CONTINUITY

Built with people.
Kept running locally.

Support local staff with practical stock, ordering and handover training. Define responsibilities, backup roles and the recurring budget needed beyond the pilot.

Local leadership is part of the operating model.

OUR COMMITMENTLocal knowledge. Shared responsibility.

Nothing lasting
is built alone.

Health authorities, facility teams and transport operators each hold a part of the answer.

We propose to work within the existing public health system, with local authorities guiding facility selection, medicine priorities and data ownership.

This is an invitation to develop a workable model together. Partnerships, funding and implementation arrangements have not yet been agreed.

Manus Essential Medicines Last-Mile Supply Chain Pilot

This website introduces a concept-stage project proposal for outer health facilities in Manus Province, Papua New Guinea. It is based on the team's working planning document dated 20 October 2026.

Purpose
Reduce days without essential medicines by connecting supply, regular delivery, stock management and quality assurance.
Proposed scope
A Lorengau hub, initially three health facilities and potential expansion to six over a proposed 24-month pilot.
Current stage
Pre-feasibility. Facilities, routes, partners, funding and implementation dates remain subject to local verification and agreement.
What comes next
Field assessment, supply agreements, route and operator checks, baseline data and a confirmed operating budget.

All illustrations are conceptual. Connections do not represent surveyed routes or selected facilities. This site presents selected ideas from an unfinished proposal, not a record of an implemented project.

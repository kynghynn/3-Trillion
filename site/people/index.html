Manus Essential Medicines Last-Mile Supply Chain Pilot

This website introduces a concept-stage project proposal for outer health facilities in Manus Province, Papua New Guinea. It is based on the team's working planning document dated 20 October 2026.

Purpose
Reduce days without essential medicines by connecting supply, regular delivery, stock management and quality assurance.
Proposed scope
A Lorengau hub, initially three health facilities and potential expansion to six over a proposed 24-month pilot.
Current stage
Pre-feasibility. Facilities, routes, partners, funding and implementation dates remain subject to local verification and agreement.
What comes next
Field assessment, supply agreements, route and operator checks, baseline data and a confirmed operating budget.

All illustrations are conceptual. Connections do not represent surveyed routes or selected facilities. This site presents selected ideas from an unfinished proposal, not a record of an implemented project.

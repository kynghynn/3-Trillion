Manus Essential Medicines Last-Mile Supply Chain Pilot
Project note | October 2026 | Concept-stage proposal

PURPOSE
Reduce days without essential medicines at outer health facilities in
Manus Province, Papua New Guinea, by connecting supply, regular delivery,
stock management and quality assurance.

PROPOSED SCOPE
A Lorengau hub, initially three health facilities and potential expansion
to six, over a proposed 24-month period from agreement activation.
Expansion depends on safety, available supply, operational readiness
and budget.

FOUR CONNECTED AREAS OF WORK
1. Regular delivery: qualified local transport, planned handovers and
   contingency arrangements.
2. Stock and ordering: reliable records, demand estimates and ordering
   routines, connected to existing systems where available.
3. Quality and handover: protection, inspection, appropriate storage and
   documented receipt of usable medicines.
4. People and continuity: practical training, clear responsibilities,
   backup roles and a recurring operating budget.

PROPOSED SEQUENCE
Months 1-4: field assessment, baseline records, supply agreements,
route and operator checks, and confirmation of operating readiness.
Months 5-24: phased operation, review and conditional expansion.
Before the pilot ends: confirm the next operating budget, responsible
staff and transport arrangements.

CURRENT STATUS
Pre-feasibility. This is not an implemented project. Facilities, routes,
partners, funding and implementation dates remain unconfirmed. Proposed
targets and scope require local verification and agreement.
